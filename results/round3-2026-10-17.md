## The long stream (L8)
- On the PC there was nothing in Apollo's log, no swap-chain events, and no System-log entries.
- The 28-minute second duplication was stable:
  - 201,415 of 201,418 frames;
  - presented → available p50 34.09, p95 34.62, max 43.65 ms;
  - longest wait 41.9 ms.
- **On the Mac,** at **05:20:21** and **05:37:51**: `Video decode unit queue overflow`, then `IDR frame request sent` and `Waiting for IDR frame`.
  - Apollo logged nothing within ±3 s of either: no capture restart, no teardown, no warning.
  - The Mac's end-of-stream stats show host processing latency of 6.1 min / **129.5 max** / 8.2 avg ms, network 2 ms, and drops of 0.02% (network) and 0.04% (jitter).
  - So the two keyframe requests weren't caused by a capture restart. A burst from the host (the 129.5 ms stall) or the network is the likelier cause, and Apollo doesn't log either at Info level.
- The Mac's six screenshots over 30 minutes show no dimming, no color drift and no freeze.

## Surprises
1. **Windows remembers SDR for the display,** so an HDR launch after an SDR one arrives with HDR off and display_device switches it on: L4 and L7. With the toggle off, that's the only switch, and it happens before the stream starts.
2. **Rebuilds still cause capture restarts:** two `ACCESS_LOST` restarts 5–10 s into each rebuilt stream, with no swap-chain event. They weren't visible on the Mac.
3. **During R1's rebuild Windows briefly reactivated the Optix G24C** (Apollo's device list says `ACTIVE`); the configuration turned it off before 6 s. The Odyssey G5 was missing from the device list after R2's rebuild, as in round 2.
4. **The Mac requested two keyframes in 30 minutes** with nothing logged on the PC; its stats show a 129.5 ms host-processing maximum.
